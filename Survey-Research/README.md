# Survey Research Portfolio

## Overview

This repository highlights my practical experience in survey research across national statistical exercises, impact evaluations, market research, academic research, and international development projects.

Over the past several years, I have contributed to the design, implementation, supervision, quality assurance, and management of large-scale surveys conducted by organizations including:

- Ghana Statistical Service
- ISSER, University of Ghana
- Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA Ghana)
- SIA360 Ltd
- Dataking Research Lab
- Jadaad Technologies
- Alhaena Research Centre
