## Project Overview

This project involved supporting research activities focused on understanding adolescent health, livelihood experiences, and community-level socioeconomic conditions.

The research applied quantitative and qualitative approaches to collect, manage, analyse, and interpret field data to generate evidence for decision-making.

- Managed research datasets and conducted data quality checks.
- Cleaned and prepared datasets for analysis.

- Supported preparation of research reports and analytical outputs.
