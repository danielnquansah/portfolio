# Ghana Socioeconomic Panel Survey (GSPS) – Wave III & IV

## Organization

Institute of Statistical, Social and Economic Research (ISSER), University of Ghana

## Project Overview

The Ghana Socioeconomic Panel Survey (GSPS) is a nationally representative longitudinal household survey designed to track changes in household welfare, employment, agriculture, education, health, and socioeconomic conditions over time. The study provides evidence for development planning, poverty analysis, and policy evaluation in Ghana.
