# Integrated Business Establishment Survey (IBES I & II) 2024

## Organization

Ghana Statistical Service (GSS)

## Project Overview

The Integrated Business Establishment Survey (IBES I & II) is a nationwide statistical exercise conducted by the Ghana Statistical Service to collect comprehensive information on business establishments across Ghana. The survey provides high-quality business statistics to support national planning, policy formulation, economic analysis, and private sector development.
