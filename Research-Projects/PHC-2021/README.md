---

## Project Overview

The 2021 Population and Housing Census was Ghana's largest nationwide statistical operation, designed to provide comprehensive demographic, social, and economic data for national planning and policy development.

As Regional Trainer and Field Supervisor, I supported the planning, training, supervision, quality assurance, and successful implementation of census field operations.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Trained approximately 60 Enumerators and Supervisors.
- Coordinated field teams across assigned districts.
- Conducted field monitoring and quality assurance.
- Reviewed completed questionnaires for consistency and completeness.
- Supported troubleshooting during field operations.
- Prepared field progress reports.
- Ensured adherence to Ghana Statistical Service protocols.
- Assisted in census logistics and operational planning.
