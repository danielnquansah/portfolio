# Monitoring & Evaluation Portfolio

## Overview

This portfolio documents my practical and academic experience in monitoring, evaluation, data collection, field supervision, research, and evidence-based reporting.

My experience spans government statistical operations, development research, impact-oriented projects, agricultural programmes, financial inclusion research, and academic research.

I have worked with organisations and projects involving Ghana Statistical Service, Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA Ghana), ISSER, SIA360 Ltd, Alhaena Research Centre, and other research and development organisations.
