# Results Frameworks

## Purpose

Results frameworks provide a structured approach for linking programme activities with outputs, outcomes, and longer-term development results.

My experience in research, field monitoring, programme assessment, and development projects has strengthened my understanding of how programme activities can be translated into measurable results.

---

## Typical Results Chain

Activities
↓
Outputs
↓
Outcomes
↓
Intermediate Outcomes
↓
Impact

---

## Example

### Development Challenge

Limited access to digital financial services among underserved populations.

### Activities

- Digital financial literacy training
- Agent network expansion
- Customer sensitisation
- Digital service demonstrations

### Outputs

- Number of people trained
- Number of communities reached
- Number of agents supported
- Number of sensitisation sessions conducted

### Outcomes

- Increased knowledge of digital financial services
- Increased adoption of digital financial products
- Increased frequency of digital transactions

### Impact

Improved financial inclusion and economic participation among underserved populations.

---

## Key Principles

- Clear causal logic
- Measurable indicators
- Appropriate data sources
- Defined reporting frequency
- Clear responsibilities
- Evidence-based decision making
